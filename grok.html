A separate page on the Nexus AI site

Chat with Grok, xAI's model, through the official xAI API. This page does not change the original Nexus AI site. The key you enter is stored only in this browser's localStorage and is sent only to api.x.ai.

About CORS

This page calls xAI directly from your browser with POST https://api.x.ai/v1/chat/completions. The browser may block that call because of CORS. If it does, a readable error will appear in the chat log. A blocked browser call does not always mean the key is invalid.
